Privacy Policy

Learn how MedGenie collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal and health-related data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MedGenie — Care at Your Fingertips . Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered healthcare services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and location details provided voluntarily.
  • Health Information: Symptoms, medical history, and health concerns shared during interactions.
  • Technical Information: Device type, browser details, IP address, and analytics data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver personalized health guidance.
  • Improve AI accuracy and system performance.
  • Enhance user experience and security.
  • Assist during emergencies if required.

We never sell your personal or medical information.

4. Data Security

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission and storage.
  • Restricted access for authorized personnel only.
  • Regular infrastructure and security audits.

5. Sharing of Information

  • With your consent for healthcare or emergency support.
  • For legal compliance if required by law.
  • With trusted infrastructure providers under strict agreements.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your stored information.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request account or data deletion.
  • Withdraw consent anytime.

Contact us at: privacy@medgenie.ai

7. AI Limitations

MedGenie provides AI-generated health information for educational and informational purposes only. It should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and analytics technologies to improve usability, monitor performance, and enhance the platform experience.

9. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and improve system quality.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes become effective immediately after publication.
